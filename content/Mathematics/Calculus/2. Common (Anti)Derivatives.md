Power rule:
- $f(x) = x^n$
- $f'(x) = nx^{n-1}$
- $F(x) = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$ if $n \neq -1$ and $\ln(|x|)+C$ otherwise.

Exponentials

|**$F(x)−C$ (Antiderivative)**|**$f(x)$ (Function)**|**$f'(x)$ (Derivative)**|
|---|---|---|
|$e^x$|$e^x$|$e^x$|
|$\frac{a^x}{\ln(a)}$|$a^x$|$a^x \ln(a)$|
|$x\ln(x) - x$|$\ln(x)$|$\frac{1}{x}$|
|$\frac{1}{\ln(a)}(x\ln(x)-x)$|$\log_a(x)$|$\frac{1}{x \ln(a)}$|

Trigonometrics

|**$F(x)−C$ (Antiderivative)**|**$f(x)$ (Function)**|**$f'(x)$ (Derivative)**|
|---|---|---|
|$-\cos(x)$|$\sin(x)$|$\cos(x)$|
|$\sin(x)$|$\cos(x)$|$-\sin(x)$|
|$\ln(\mid \sec(x) \mid)$|$\tan(x)$|$\sec^2(x)$|
|$-\ln(\mid \csc(x) + \cot(x) \mid)$|$\csc(x)$|$-\csc(x)\cot(x)$|
|$\ln(\mid \sec(x) + \tan(x) \mid)$|$\sec(x)$|$\sec(x)\tan(x)$|
|$-\ln(\mid \sin(x) \mid)$|$\cot(x)$|$-\csc^2(x)$|

Chain rule for antiderivatives

- $f(x)=g(ax+b)$
- $F(x)=\frac{1}{a}F(ax+b)+C$
