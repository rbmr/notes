If we have a partition $\lambda$ of an integer $n$, such that:
$$
\lambda = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \dots, \lambda_k)
$$
where $\sum_{i=1}^{k} \lambda_i = n$, then the sum of squares is defined as:
$$
SSQ(\lambda) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \lambda_i^2
$$
For some fixed $k$, the minimum sum of squares happens when the partitions are as equal as possible, the maximum sum of squares happens when the partitions are as imbalanced as possible.
